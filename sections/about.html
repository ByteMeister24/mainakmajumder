About me

I am a doctoral researcher specializing in Industrial Automation and Cyber-Physical Production Systems (CPPS), with a focus on semantic information modelling, industrial interoperability, and software architectures for Industry 4.0 systems. My work explores how modern software engineering and model-driven approaches can be applied to industrial automation, particularly through technologies such as OPC UA information modelling and IEC 61499-based distributed control systems. I am particularly interested in developing methods and tools for creating interoperable digital representations of manufacturing systems and integrating legacy industrial assets into cyber-physical production environments. My research sits at the intersection of industrial communication standards, manufacturing system modelling, and software architecture for smart manufacturing systems.
